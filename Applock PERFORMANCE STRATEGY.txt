Optimization Goals

---

ONLY.

6. COMPOSE PERFORMANCE STRATEGY

Problem

Bad Compose architecture causes:

unnecessary recomposition

jank

dropped frames



---

Core Compose Rules

Use Immutable UI State

Good:

data class UiState(...)


---

Avoid Massive State Objects

Split states by feature.


---

Use Stable Models

Prevent unnecessary recomposition.


---

Example Flow

↓

↓

---

Bad Example

↓
