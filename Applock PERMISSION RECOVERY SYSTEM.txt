PERMISSION RECOVERY SYSTEM — Advanced Android App Lock

Version 1.0

This document defines:

permission monitoring

permission failure detection

recovery flows

OEM-specific recovery handling

user guidance system

automatic protection degradation handling


Goal:

detect failures quickly
recover protection smoothly
avoid silent security failure

Important reality:

Android permissions are NOT permanent.

OEMs and users may:

disable accessibility

revoke overlay permission

kill background services

restrict battery usage

disable notifications


Your app must:

continuously verify protection health

instead of assuming permissions always work.


---

1. RECOVERY SYSTEM PHILOSOPHY

Core Principle

Never allow:

silent protection failure

If protection weakens:

detect immediately

warn clearly

guide recovery

reduce risk



---

Security Health Model

Protection health should behave like:

State	Meaning
