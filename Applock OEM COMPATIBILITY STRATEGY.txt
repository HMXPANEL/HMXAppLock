OEM COMPATIBILITY STRATEGY — Advanced Android App Lock

Version 1.0

This document defines:

OEM-specific Android behavior

background restriction handling

service survival strategy

permission reliability

battery optimization handling

recovery flows

device-specific onboarding


Goal:

maximize protection reliability
across real Android devices

Important reality:

Android is NOT consistent across OEMs.

Samsung, Xiaomi, Oppo, Vivo, Huawei, OnePlus: all modify Android differently.

A security app that works perfectly on Pixel may:

fail silently on Xiaomi

lose overlays on Oppo

get killed on Vivo


This document exists to prevent:

silent protection failure


---

Core Principle

Problem

kills background services

blocks auto-start

suspends accessibility behavior

HyperOS

Common Failure Example

↓
